EtsyHunt Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

EtsyHunt is a free Chrome extension for local Etsy niche research. It is designed so your research data stays on your device. It does not require an EtsyHunt account and does not sell your data.

Data stored locally

The extension may store the following in Chrome local storage on your computer:

  • Seed keywords you enter
  • Settings you configure
  • Research results from runs you start (keywords, listing metadata, scores, activity logs)
  • Run history and timestamps

Clearing extension data or uninstalling EtsyHunt removes this data.

Website content accessed during research

When you start a run, EtsyHunt may open tabs and read publicly visible content from:

  • etsy.com — keyword discovery, search snapshots, listing audits
  • members.erank.com — only for the optional legacy eRank workflow, and only if you are logged into eRank in Chrome

That content is processed locally to build your niche report. EtsyHunt does not upload those results to an EtsyHunt server.

Downloads

The extension may save HTML reports and exported JSON logs to your Downloads folder when a run finishes, when a report is generated, or when you click Export.

Permissions

PermissionPurpose

scriptingHelpers for the optional eRank workflow

downloadsSave reports and exported logs
Host access (Etsy / eRank)Read pages only during research runs you start

What we do not do

  • No EtsyHunt signup requirement
  • No selling or renting personal data
  • No advertising use of your research data
  • No tracking of browsing outside runs you start
  • No analytics or telemetry inside the extension itself

Analytics on this website

This privacy policy page (not the extension) uses Google Analytics to understand visits to this page — for example, approximate location, device/browser type, and how visitors arrived here. Google Analytics uses cookies and may collect your IP address. This applies only to your browser visiting this webpage; the EtsyHunt Chrome extension itself does not send any analytics or telemetry.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or by using a browser/extension that blocks tracking scripts. See Google's Privacy Policy for details on how Google handles this data.

Third parties

Etsy and eRank are independent services. Your use of those sites is governed by their own policies. Google Analytics (used only on this webpage) is governed by Google's privacy policy, linked above.
